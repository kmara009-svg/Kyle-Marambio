About Me

My name is Kyle Marambio, and I am currently completing a Bachelor of Exercise and Sport Science at Swinburne University of Technology, having previously completed a Diploma of Health Science.

I am passionate about understanding how the human body moves, adapts, and recovers — and applying that knowledge to help individuals perform at their best. My goal is to pursue a career in physiotherapy, combining clinical expertise with a genuine love for sport and movement.

Outside of study, I'm an athlete who practices what I study — training regularly and staying active in the sports science community.

I am currently completing placement at Methodist Ladies College, supervising and working alongside strength and conditioning coaches to help deliver exercise sessions and programs for elite adolescent athletes.
